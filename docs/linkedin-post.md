## POSTING TIPS FOR MAXIMUM REACH

1. **Post at 8–9am or 5–6pm** on Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday for best LinkedIn reach
2. **Add a personal photo or screenshot** of the platform — posts with images get 3x more views
3. **End every post with a question** — it drives comments which boost algorithm reach
4. **Engage for 30 min after posting** — reply to every comment in the first hour
5. **Tag relevant people** — investors, customers, or collaborators you know on LinkedIn
6. **Use 3–5 hashtags max** — LinkedIn penalises posts with too many
7. **Post version 1 first** — it's the strongest for a launch announcement
